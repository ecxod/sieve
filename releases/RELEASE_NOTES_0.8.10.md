## Highlights

- Checks the ManageSieve connection before loading the Inbox rule editor's
  **Add to Sieve script** selector.
- Connects an offline account automatically, verifies the resulting connection,
  and updates the account's online indicator before retrieving server scripts.
- Leaves an already connected account untouched and reports a clear error when
  the connection cannot be established.
- Applies the behavior consistently in the Thunderbird extension and Windows
  application, with a focused connection-order test.
