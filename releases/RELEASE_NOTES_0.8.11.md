## Highlights

- Applies **Run Sieve now** to the explicitly selected Inbox message after
  rechecking its identity and UIDVALIDITY; the action never issues `EXPUNGE`.
- Shows possible existing sender, domain, recipient, and subject rules with
  nearby source context in a separate read-only field in the Inbox rule editor.
- Turns successful syntax-check buttons green and then gradually fades them
  back to their normal color.
- Adds **Dark Light** and black **AMOLED** appearance variants with clearer card
  and button borders to the Windows application.
- Adds a Debian/Devuan package recipe without systemd or maintainer scripts.
